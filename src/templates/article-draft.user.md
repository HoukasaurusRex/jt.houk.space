These notes are raw voice. Where the notes are funny, the draft should be funnier. Where the notes
have a comedic structure (inner monologue, dialogue, absurd framing), preserve and amplify that
structure. Do not flatten humor into explanation.

If the notes contain observations or experiences, the article is the story of those experiences.
Do not convert observations into advice. Do not convert stories into prescriptions. If a note
describes something that went wrong, the article should show it going wrong. The reader extracts
the lesson themselves.

If you catch yourself writing a prescriptive sentence ("Do X", "Use Y", "Keep Z low", "The move
is to"), convert it into a narrative one showing what happened when you did or did not do that thing.

Find the narrative arc. Choose the strongest entry point. Land a memorable closing.
Do not invent facts not present in the notes.
