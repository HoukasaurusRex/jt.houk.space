## Projects

Two PRs moved forward today. I opened PR #1971 for LRX-37242, adding `include_deleted` to `SuggestProductsRequest` on the typey side,
and reviewed and approved Vicky's PR #6835 for LRX-20844, which fixes the split promo error translation. Clean work on her end, easy approval.

The bigger story today was an architectural dead end. While investigating how to wire up deleted entity filtering to the suggest query,
I hit a fundamental problem: deleting an entity removes it from the search index entirely. There is no deleted record to filter on because the record
is simply gone. I pulled my findings together, presented them to the team, and we are now discussing what to do next. What stings a little is that
there had apparently already been a discussion about not supporting deleted product and customer search, and that context never made it back to me
despite my mentioning this work at standup for several days running. A lesson in making sure decisions get surfaced to whoever is doing the work,
not just the people who were in the room. For now I have documented the investigation on the ticket and backlogged it. I also picked up LRX-36444,
the research ticket around sales being created as pending without attributes, and started poking at it.

## Peer Feedback - Insaf

### What is insaf doing well?

When I left for time off mid-project, I handed Insaf two genuinely tricky CDC stream tasks: migrating the `cash_movement` type column from `tinyint`
to `tinyint(4)` so BigQuery would stop misreading it as a boolean, and adding `retailer_id` hydration to the `register_open_sequence` stream.
He resolved both, along with related pipeline stream updates, a backfill, and null `retailer_id` filtering during that backfill.
Those fixes collectively unblocked the entire Cash Movement reporting pipeline. I came back to a problem that was largely solved rather than one
waiting on me, which is exactly what you hope for when you hand off work.

### What should insaf do more or less of?

The day we had the Cash Movement Report onboarding scheduled, Insaf messaged at 2pm to push it to the next day because another task had come up.
That kind of conflict is completely normal, but flagging it earlier in the day would let the other person redirect their afternoon rather than holding
it open. As Insaf takes on more independent ownership of work, getting ahead of scheduling friction early will make cross-team coordination a lot
smoother for everyone involved.
