## Projects

Leo's PR #1891 for LRX-37461, fixing the `sale_total` calculation precision mismatch with MySQL, got my approval today. After yesterday's conversation
about three-decimal currencies, we decided against adding that support for now, which is the right call to make deliberately rather than discover
accidentally in production. Zhijie's PR #15472 for deprecating `@import` with a stylelint rule in business-components is in review.
I ran the SCSS migration tool against the repo and have been digging into edge cases around how Lightspeed modules are affected by the deprecation.
Import migrations tend to look mechanical until you hit a module that relies on a load order quirk, so the research is worth doing carefully before we
wave this through.

The summary filter context i18n interpolation issue is proving stubborn. I have confirmed it is reproducible in both dev and prod environments behind
the feature flag, which at least rules out a local environment problem. The investigation is going deeper into the code today.
Roberto also flagged LRX-36444, a research ticket around sales being created as pending without attributes. I want to take a first pass at the
monolith today if time allows, even a cursory look often surfaces enough to know whether the root cause is shallow or worth a dedicated session.
