## Projects

The release schedule had teeth today, and we met them. Three sales history hotfixes needed to go from zero to merged before the release window closed,
and that is exactly what happened. LRX-39113 resolves a non-deterministic sort order that was surfacing when sales involved multiple payment types,
the kind of bug that is maddening to reproduce because the wrong answer looks plausible until you stare at it long enough. LRX-39116,
which I had opened yesterday after catching the silent filter drop on `saleTotal: "0.00"`, is now fully fixed and merged. The third, LRX-39202,
gates the sale total filter and sort properly and also corrects the payment type display order on descending sorts. All three went through the full
cycle today: investigation, scoping, ticketing, development, and merge.

Tomorrow I need to review the design changes Ivana made for the new filters epic and assess how they affect the technical discovery already underway.
Design changes at this stage are not a problem so long as we catch them before the engineering tickets get too far ahead of the actual requirements.
