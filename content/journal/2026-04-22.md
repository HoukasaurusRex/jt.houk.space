## Projects

Digging into LRX-37483, which covers sale total filter edge cases, I ran into some genuinely tricky locale number formatting behavior.
The culprit was complex locale formats like `de-DE`, where `1.000,50` is a perfectly valid representation of one thousand and a half.
That kind of inversion of the period and comma conventions used in `en-US` is exactly the sort of thing that slips through until you go looking for
it. I caught several edge cases in PR #15546 and am working through them now. This one sits at the intersection of the deleted product search work and
localization, which makes it a good reminder that number parsing is never as boring as it looks.
