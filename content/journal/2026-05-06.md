## Projects

I circled back to Zhijie's work today, covering two PRs in webregister. The first was PR #6844, adding the option to set best price as base price for
line item pricing calculations. Pricing logic is one of those areas where small decisions ripple outward in ways that aren't always obvious at review

I'd left blocking comments on this one earlier in the week, so this was a follow-up look to see where things landed. Large sale performance is the
kind of problem that hides well in normal conditions and only surfaces when a merchant actually needs it to work, which makes the review investment
more than worth it.
