## Projects

I picked up LRX-41208 today, which tracks a nasty little problem: sale-level discounts are having their tax components stripped somewhere in the
pipeline, which breaks QBO sync downstream. The investigation is touching a lot of surface area at once. I am tracing the issue across webregister,
the accounting integration, graphter, and the monolith, which is the kind of multi-service archaeology that requires patience and good note-taking.
No root cause nailed down yet, but the picture is getting clearer.
