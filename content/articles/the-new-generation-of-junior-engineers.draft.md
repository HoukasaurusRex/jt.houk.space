Juniors aren't here to contribute at the level of Senior Engineers, they're here to learn and grow as criticallly thinking engineers
who will make the architectural decisions of the future, but right now they're working with a good-enough universal answer sheet
talking over their shoulders at all times.
We can modify repository documentation, AGENTS.md files, and automated pipelines to mitigate risk and elevate code quality of
agentic contributions to a satisfactory level, but to what end? We get a constant stream of good-enough code following existing patterns,
but since when has a codebase ever not needed to evolving? At a higher level, most mature companies will at some point push the
software industry forward by developing new patterns and architectures to solve unique problems at scale. When we get to the problems no one
has solved before, we look to the highly experienced and the highly creative to invent new solutions together.
